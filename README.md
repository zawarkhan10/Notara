# Notara

### A better way to build, discover, and connect.

Notara is a developer-focused platform built around a simple idea: making it easier for people to share what they build, discover other projects, and connect with passionate people in tech.

Designed with beginners in mind, Notara aims to create a more approachable experience for developers at every stage of their journey.
https://trynotara.netlify.app

## What We're Building

- 💻 A platform centered around developers and their projects
- 🌱 A more beginner-friendly experience
- 🔎 A way to discover projects and new ideas
- 🤝 Opportunities to connect with like-minded builders

## Our Vision

To create a welcoming space where developers can showcase their work, discover inspiration, and grow alongside a community of passionate builders.
